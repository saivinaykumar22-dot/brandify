FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know before launching your brand with Brandify.

What is Brandify?

Brandify gives creators the infrastructure to launch their own branded products while Brandify handles the operational side such as sourcing, manufacturing, private labeling, packaging, fulfillment, and shipping.

How does Brandify work?

Choose a product, customize it, build your brand identity, set your selling price, and launch. When customers place orders, Brandify handles the fulfillment process.

Can I customize the products?

Yes. Brandify allows creators to customize eligible products and apply their own brand identity, including branding and packaging options where available.

Do I need to manage inventory?

Brandify manages the product and fulfillment infrastructure. Creators can focus on building their brand, selling products, and serving their customers.

How does fulfillment work?

Once an order is submitted for fulfillment, the product moves through the required process including manufacturing, labeling, packaging, fulfillment, and shipping.

How do I get started?

Choose your product, build your brand, complete the setup, and launch your brand through Brandify.
